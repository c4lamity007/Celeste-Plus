# Celeste+ fixed-tilt hand wall — 2026-09-20

Published release: `celeste-plus-20260920-hand-wall-01` (Fly v98).

## Changes

- All hand placements use a gentler horizontal cylinder: center depth 0.82,
  radius 1.64, center behind the eye at Z=0.82. The projected grip still matches
  the editor position. Changing vertical position changes only translation,
  never yaw, pitch, roll, size or the selected display context.
- The fixed vertical tilt is the previous non-sword -10 degrees. Swords retain
  their smooth left/right in-plane lean, without the height-dependent pitch
  introduced by the preceding sword-facing update.
- Flat-item facing is resolved from the actual selected model, not a sword tag.
  Its effective first-person rotation, including the different left-hand rules,
  is corrected while retaining its authored in-plane roll. This covers ordinary
  generated/handheld items and the crossbow's different X-oriented basis.
- Blocks, special renderers such as shields, mixed/unsupported models and true
  3D models retain their authored model orientation under the common wall pose.
  All items retain their physical display context so crossing the screen center
  cannot switch model translations or handed transforms.
- The public model-state probe never renders or accesses private game fields.
  It caches per physical hand, stack/components/count, model identity and holder,
  refreshing dynamic selection at most once per world tick. Work is bounded to
  eight layers and 2,048 quads; unsupported cases safely keep authored facing.
  Disable, world change and leaving the editor clear the cache.

No hand shader, fog, glow, slot, item-use, networking or auth logic is changed.
Vanilla swing/equip animation, editor snapping and size controls are retained.
