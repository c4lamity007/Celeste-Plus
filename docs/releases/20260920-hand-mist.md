## Changes

- The mist uses a tight half-resolution prefilter followed by a dense,
  quarter-resolution separable blur, replacing the widely spaced samples that
  left repeated contours around diagonal item edges.
- Only the softened fog silhouette has temporal history. Its 85 ms exponential
  time constant follows elapsed time, not frame count. The real item, animated
  material coating and clean outline remain current-frame sharp.
- Ping-pong history uses 16-bit scalar coverage packed into RGBA8 RG channels;
  its linear decoder remains valid under bilinear filtering. This avoids an
  8-bit feedback floor retaining faint fog indefinitely at high frame rates.
- History resets for item-type, perspective/eligibility, world/player/screen,
  handedness, settings, render-target and size changes, on capture failure and
  after a frame gap above 250 ms. Disable, reload and disconnect release targets.
- Darker outer wisps, irregular bright inner patches and the flowing blade coat
  are preserved. No circular particles were added. Saved Particles mode remains
  the fog effect; Outline and Glow retain their original rendering paths.
