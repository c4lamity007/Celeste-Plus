## Changes

- Hand Shaders keeps the clean outline and adds a wider, animated luminous mist,
  irregular bright patches and a tinted flowing blade coating. The final version
  contains no discrete circular particles. Outer fog is darker in color and fades
  smoothly; brighter patches remain near the blade. Original item luminance
  supplies the coating's texture; original material alpha is preserved.
- The existing Particles mode name is retained for saved-config compatibility.
  Outline and Glow output remain unchanged. The effect still uses two half-size
  blur passes and one composite, with no world particles or readback.
- Hand Models supports independent saved hand sizes from 50% to 150%, in 5%
  scroll steps. Dragged/hovered/selected hands are the wheel targets. Reset restores
  the default positions and both sizes.
- Positions snap to a 2% normalized grid with a small dead band. The grips follow
  a forward circular arc, with continuous inward yaw and a fixed slight tilt.
  Vanilla first-person display contexts select the appropriate screen-side
  facing without negative scale or changing the player's physical hand.
